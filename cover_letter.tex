% 2. THE PITCH (Significance & Fit)
% Do not just copy your abstract. Explain the "big picture" importance.
In this manuscript, we explore alignment between NASA science plans and scientific community consensus using Natural Language Processing. We created a baseline of 4 Decadal Surveys from 2001 to 2007 and measured similarities between the baseline and NASA science plans. Our key two key finds are that alignment is shown to decrease over time and Mission directorate led science plans have higher alignment than NASA led strategic plans. We believe our analysis directly relates to the aim of \textit{Space Policy} to provide discussion and analysis of space activities in their political, economic, legal, and social context and is therefore of interest to your readers.
